Method in brief
  • Data: volunteer (eTryvoga) oblast-level alert records from the Vadimkin dataset; records longer than 12 h dropped; overlapping intervals merged.
  • Target: alert at any moment of the hour [τ+L, τ+L+1h), for L = 1, 2, 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72 h.
  • No leakage: features use only hours that ended by τ; climatology is shifted back by whole days so its newest hour ended by τ; the model is refit every 7 days on labels already observed at the refit time.
  • Test: issue times (Kyiv), days, identical for every lead.
  • CI: 500–2000 bootstrap resamples of calendar days.
